# ChhatraLekha v1.0.0 announcement draft

## Short social post

ChhatraLekha v1.0.0 is live: a multilingual academic identity and verification platform built to help students, parents, educators, institutions, and verifiers authenticate, verify, and empower academic journeys.

The release brings the complete ChhatraLekha rebrand, a unified student identity experience, tamper-evident credentials, QR verification, consent-aware sharing, institution workflows, anomaly detection, multilingual access, and guided AI assistance.

Explore the release: https://github.com/kumaribaishnabidas/ChhatraLekha/releases/tag/v1.0.0

## Announcement email

**Subject:** ChhatraLekha v1.0.0 is now available

Hello,

We are pleased to announce the first stable release of **ChhatraLekha** — a unified academic identity and verification platform designed for students, parents, teachers, institutions, and credential verifiers.

### What is new in v1.0.0

- A complete ChhatraLekha identity, including the supplied logo and a consistent Royal Blue, Orange, White, and Ivory visual system.
- Student identity and digital APAAR workflows with privacy-conscious QR and token verification.
- Credential wallet, consent-aware sharing, activity history, uploads, results, and academic records.
- Institution, teacher, parent, verifier, and AICTE-oriented workspaces for day-to-day academic operations.
- CSV intake, anomaly detection, impact analysis, reporting, and regulatory workflow support.
- Multilingual navigation across 13 supported locales, with voice and accessibility-oriented experiences.
- Backend, Android, frontend, container, and CI improvements for a more reliable release foundation.

### Built for trust

ChhatraLekha is designed around authenticated records, limited-purpose sharing, transparent verification, and clear separation between student, family, educator, institution, and verifier workflows.

Thank you to everyone helping shape a more accessible and trustworthy academic identity experience.

— The ChhatraLekha team
